How do job applicants experience AI-driven interviews, and how do system behaviors shape their trust, anxiety, agency, and perceptions of the organization? My research examines the limitations of existing AI interviewers and explores how interactive features, feedback, transparency, and human-like communication cues can improve the interview experience. Using interviews, surveys, system design, and controlled experiments, I aim to develop AI-mediated interviewing systems that are more responsive, supportive, and respectful of applicants.
