Most people who use AI-powered tools have no technical background. What mental models do they form? How accurate are those models, and how do inaccurate models lead to over-trust, under-trust, or misuse?

Using surveys, diary studies, and interviews, we mapped the landscape of lay AI mental models and identified design patterns that help calibrate user expectations.
