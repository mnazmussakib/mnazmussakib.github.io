How can reflection between meetings help learners remain focused, aligned, and prepared for their next collaboration? My research explores how AI-generated prompts can encourage learners to reflect on previous discussions, recognize gaps in their understanding, and plan their next steps. Using theory-driven system design and controlled user studies, I aim to develop adaptive reflection tools that support deeper thinking and sustained collaborative learning.

Keywords: AI-supported reflection · Collaborative learning · Adaptive prompting · Human-AI interaction
