More explanation isn't always better. This project investigates what kinds of AI explanations actually help users make better decisions — and which ones overwhelm, confuse, or paradoxically reduce trust.

We run think-aloud studies and A/B experiments across explanation formats (saliency maps, counterfactuals, natural language) to identify when transparency works and when it backfires.
