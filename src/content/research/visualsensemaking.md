How do people collaboratively interpret complex visual information, and when does facilitation help them develop a shared understanding? My research examines how groups move from observing visual evidence to discussing, interpreting, and resolving competing explanations. Using interaction analysis, dialogue coding, and controlled user studies, we aim to design AI facilitators that support productive discussion without interrupting or replacing participants’ reasoning.

Keywords: Visual sensemaking · Social Co-construction · AI facilitation · Interaction analysis · Human-AI collaboration
