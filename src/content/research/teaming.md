When people work alongside AI systems on decisions that really matter — medical triage, legal review, crisis response — how do they divide cognitive labor? When do they defer to the AI, and when do they push back?

This project uses contextual inquiry and controlled experiments to map the dynamics of human-AI collaboration, with the goal of designing AI teammates that complement rather than displace human judgment.
